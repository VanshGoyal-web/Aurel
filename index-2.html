A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A good place
to begin.

Which case size is right for me?+

A 36 mm case gives a more understated, classic presence; 40 mm feels a little bolder. Wrist shape and personal preference matter more than a single rule. Atelier editions have their own proportions.

Leather or a steel bracelet?+

Leather brings warmth and a softer, dressier feel. Steel has a clean, versatile presence. Try our watch finder for a recommendation based on your style.

How should I look after a watch?+

Wipe the case with a soft, dry cloth, keep leather away from prolonged moisture, and avoid strong magnets and impacts. Follow the actual manufacturer's care and water-resistance guidance.
